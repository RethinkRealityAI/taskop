# Client logo sources

Each logo was taken from the client's own website and normalised with sharp (trimmed, transparent
background preserved, capped width for crisp 2x rendering). They are displayed in grayscale and
lift to full colour on hover.

- **city-of-toronto.png** — https://www.toronto.ca/wp-content/themes/cot/img/logo-print.svg
  (official print wordmark, rendered from SVG at 1200px wide).
- **scago.png** — https://www.sicklecellanemia.ca/ header logo.
- **escf.png** — https://www.escfederation.eu/ logo badge; the white JPEG background was knocked
  out so the mark sits cleanly on tinted surfaces.
- **gansid.png** — https://inheritedblooddisorders.world/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/logo-FINAL-2023.png
- **scaon.png** — https://www.scaon.org/ (Wix-hosted mark); the opaque light-grey disc behind the
  artwork was knocked out so it reads on white.
- **shea-allnaturals.png** — https://www.allnaturalscosmetics.com/brand/logo-blue-horizontal.png

Logos are the trademarks of their respective owners and are used here to identify TaskOp's clients.
