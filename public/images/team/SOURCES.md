# Team photo sources

All files are 800x1000 (4:5) JPEG, produced with sharp (`fit: cover, position: attention`, quality 82, mozjpeg).
Collected 2026-09-09. LinkedIn was unreachable without authentication (HTTP 999), so all photos come from
organisation websites or press pages. The original Taskop WordPress photos (`lanre3.jpg`, `pam_2.jpg`,
`sam_bio.jpg`, `dapo_bio.jpg`, `victor_icon.png`) are referenced by the Wayback Machine's copy of the old
team page, but the image files themselves were never archived.
